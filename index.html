Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch: no ads, no subscription, no tracking.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Tendroll works entirely on your device. There is no account and no server.

Does it delete anything without asking?

No. Photos you swipe up wait in Tendroll's Trash. Deleting asks iOS for confirmation, and iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What if the app closes in the middle?

You continue exactly where you stopped.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes, including photos stored only in iCloud.

Can I give access to only some photos?

Yes. Tendroll then sees only the photos you picked, and you can add more at any time.

Which languages?

English and Russian.
